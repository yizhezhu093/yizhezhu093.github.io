Research

Randomness. Structure. Learning.

01

Random matrices &
high-dimensional probability

Spectral limits, extreme eigenvalues, and concentration for random matrices, graphs, and tensors, with an emphasis on sparse and dependent structures.

02

High-dimensional
inference & learning

Spectral and iterative algorithms for community detection and low-rank recovery, and connections between random matrix theory, kernel methods, and deep learning.

03

Differential privacy &
synthetic data

Statistical and computational foundations of private synthetic data: privacy–utility tradeoffs, adaptation to data structure, and fundamental limits.

Background

About me

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Southern California.

From January 2022 to June 2024, I was a Visiting Assistant Professor at UC Irvine and a postdoc in the NSF–Simons Collaboration on the Theoretical Foundations of Deep Learning, mentored by Roman Vershynin. In Fall 2021, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Simons Laufer Mathematical Sciences Institute.

I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from UC San Diego in 2021, advised by Ioana Dumitriu, and my bachelor’s degree in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
